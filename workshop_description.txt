[h1]Vanilla Eyes[/h1]

512x512 eye colors in 12 shades for the vanilla bodies, picked from a dropdown on the character creation screen. All art was handmade in Krita. Tried doing 256, but they all looked even more unnerving than these.

[b]Open Disclosure. I utilize several daemons through [i]opencode[/i] in my workflow for formatting, code analysis, debugging, and testing. [i]I'm not concerned about your opinion on that[/i], so keep it to yourself.[/b]

[h1]Contents[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Eye colors in 12 shades[/b] - Amber, Black, Blue, Brown, Gray, Green, Hazel, Light Amber, Light Blue, Light Brown, Light Green, Light Hazel - with separate male/female fits and per-skin-tone texture slots, eventually. Framework is there, I just have to put pen to pad.
[*][b]Creation dropdown[/b] - pick your color right in the body section. Hover previews, the randomize button rolls eyes too, and outfit/gender/skintone changes keep your pick.
[/list]

[h1]Standalone[/h1]
Eyes are ordinary clothing worn at the mod's own hydeyes:eyes slot, which nothing else in the game uses - no glasses conflict, no masks, no makeup kits. Single-item, so the engine itself enforces one pair at a time. They ride the creation desc into the game, then persist and sync like any other worn item. No requirements, no known incompatibilities, but may need small patches to show on other mod's 3d paperdoll models.

[h1]Functions[/h1]
All of this lives in [b]42/media/lua[/b]. Eye entries themselves are registered with [b]hydEyesData.AddEye[/b] (see the For Modders section).
[list]
[*][b]hydEyesData.AddEye[/b] - registers one eye color: male/female items, per-skin-tone texture count, and an optional short code label. The creation dropdown lists entries in registration order.
[*][b]hydEyesData.itemFor[/b] - picks the wearer's item for an entry: sex fit first, then the skin-tone texture clamped to what the item carries.
[*][b]Creation dropdown[/b] - built under the body divider on first open. Hovering previews on the avatar, picking wears immediately, the dice rolls a color too, and outfit/gender/skintone changes re-apply the pick.
[*][b]Play handoff[/b] - the preview copy is stripped before the world loads (so the engine never trips over it) and each spawned player is dressed in their recorded pick - per-player in splitscreen.
[*][b]hydEyesData.wearOn[/b] - the post-spawn dress call above; also reusable by other mods.
[/list]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
[list]
[*]None. This mod is fully standalone. Should work on most retextures unless they put the eyes in a strange spot.
[*][b]Dedicated servers must install this mod too.[/b] Clients need the textures.
[/list]

[h1]For Modders - Add your own eyes![/h1]
This pack is a framework: register entries with hydEyesData.AddEye and they appear in the creation dropdown automatically. Full guide with copy-paste snippets ships with the mod as [b]WIKI.md[/b]. Also available on the Github.

[h1]Conditional compat patches[/h1]
No extra requirements, each activates only if its target mod is present.
[list]
[*][b]GridCharacterHub[/b] - the health-view model keeps your eyes instead of dropping them. Uses a [b]keepOnModel[/b] wrapper so the build keeps the eye slot, plus a per-panel render hook ([b]reInjectGridEyes[/b]) that re-wears your eyes onto the model's desc after every rebuild.
[*][b]ItIsOfInterestToMe[/b] - your ID card photo wears your eyes ([b]copyLiveEyes[/b] live plus a per-card snapshot so rebuilds survive reloads), and your own card prints the matching eye color in that mod's 3-letter codes ([b]wornEyeCode[/b]): Amber AMB, Black BLA, Blue BLU, Brown BRO, Gray GRY, Green GRN, Hazel HAZ, with Light shades printing as their base color. Random NPC cards stay vanilla.
[/list]

[h1]Other Compatibility[/h1]
[list]
[*]Multiplayer and splitscreen are [b]untested[/b] - single-player is verified. The code paths are set up to work (per-player picks keyed by player index, engine-synced worn clothing), but neither has been confirmed in a live session yet.
[*]Works with all vanilla skintones, and even some mods that change the body model itself, like Better Bodies.
[/list]
