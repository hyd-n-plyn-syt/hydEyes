description=[h1]Vanilla Eyes[/h1]
description=
description=512px Eyes in 12 different shades for the vanilla bodies and textures, with selectable eye shape, eye height, and lash length. All art was handmade in Krita. Tried doing 256 versions, but they all looked even more unnerving than these.
description=
description=[b]Open Disclosure[/b]. I utilize several daemons through [i]opencode[/i] in my workflow for formatting, code analysis, debugging, and testing. [b][i]I'm not concerned about your opinion on that[/i][/b], so keep it to yourself.
description=
description=[h1]Contents[/h1]
description=[quote][list]
description=[*][b]Eye Colors in 12 shades[/b] - Amber, Black, Blue, Brown, Gray, Green, Hazel, Light Amber, Light Blue, Light Brown, Light Green, and Light Hazel.
description=[*][b]Eye Shapes[/b] - 4 shapes selected from the [b]Eye Shape[/b] dropdown.
description=[*][b]Eye Heights[/b] - [b]Low[/b], [b]Normal[/b] (default), and [b]High[/b], so the eye sits where you want on the face.
description=[*][b]Lash lengths[/b] - [b]None[/b], [b]Short[/b], and [b]Long[/b]. Lashes are a separate layer drawn over the eye color.
description=[*][b]Bloodshot eyes[/b] - A separate overlay that shows up while you are drunk, badly sick, or furious, and fades again once you sober up or recover. It deepens in up to six stacked layers: intoxication over 30% adds one, over 50% a second, over 70% a third; sickness at 75% adds one and at 90% a second; anger at 75% adds one. The three can combine up to the six-layer cap. It follows the shape and height you picked, per eye.
description=[*][b]Infected eyes[/b] - a yellow overlay that tracks zombie infection, the 0-100% value that eventually turns you. It deepens in five steps (10-20%, 21-40%, 41-60%, 61-80%, 81-100%), and sickness at 90% or worse adds a sixth layer. The infected layers are drawn [i]under[/i] the bloodshot ones, so yellow and red mix into a sicklier color when both are active. It follows the shape and height you picked, per eye.
description=[*][b]Layered rendering[/b] - Face -> Eyes -> Lashes -> Infection Overlays -> Bloodshot Overlays. Changing one layer never replaces another, and overlay layers occur as the change does.
description=[*][b]Split left and right eyes[/b] - each color ships its own left-eye and right-eye art, worn on two separate slots.
description=[*][b]Heterochromia trait[/b] - A 1-point disadvantage that gives a character two different eye colors. Pick the trait and the single dropdown becomes [b]Left Eye Color[/b] and [b]Right Eye Color[/b]; the two can never match, not even from the randomize button.
description=[*][b]Creation dropdowns[/b] - pick your eyes in an [b]Eyes[/b] section of their own, above Body. Hover previews, the randomize button rolls eyes too, and outfit/gender/skintone changes keep your picks.
description=[/list][/quote]
description=
description=[h1]Standalone[/h1]
description=Eyes, lashes and both overlays are ordinary clothing worn at the mod's own hydeyes:lefteye and hydeyes:righteye slots, plus hydeyes:leftlash and hydeyes:rightlash for the lash layers, hydeyes:leftinfected (with hydeyes:leftinfected2, 3, 4, 5 and 6, plus the right-side equivalents) for the stacked infected layers, and hydeyes:leftbloodshot (with hydeyes:leftbloodshot2, 3, 4, 5 and 6, plus the right-side equivalents) for the stacked bloodshot layers - nothing else in the game uses them since we're adding them, so no glasses conflict, no masks, no makeup kits. They ride the creation desc into the game, then persist and sync like any other worn item. No requirements, no known incompatibilities, but may need small patches to show on other mod's 3d paperdoll models since they don't utilize the default one, usually.
description=
description=[h1]Requirements[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]None[/b]. This mod is fully standalone. Should work on most retextures unless they put the eyes in a strange spot. Now that eyes have height settings, should work on most of the odd ones, too.
description=[*][b]Dedicated servers [i]must[/i] install this mod too.[/b] Clients need the textures.
description=[/list]

description=[h1]For Modders - Add your own eyes![/h1]
description=This pack is a framework: register entries with hydEyesData.AddEye and they appear in the creation dropdown automatically. Full guide with copy-paste snippets ships with the mod as [b]WIKI.md[/b]. Also available on the [url=https://github.com]Github[/url].
description=
description=[h1]Compatibility Patches[/h1]
description=No extra requirements, each activates only if its target mod is present, otherwise the patches get ignored.
description=[quote][list]
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com]GridCharacterHub[/url] - The health-view model keeps your eyes, lashes and both overlay sets instead of dropping them, on every slot. Uses a [b]keepOnModel[/b] wrapper so the build keeps those slots, plus a per-panel render hook ([b]reInjectGridEyes[/b]) that re-wears them onto the model's desc after every rebuild.
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com]ItIsOfInterestToMe[/url] - Your ID card photo wears your eyes, lashes and both overlay sets ([b]copyLiveEyes[/b] live plus a per-card snapshot so rebuilds survive reloads), and your own card prints the matching eye color in that mod's 3-letter codes ([b]wornEyeCodes[/b]): Amber AMB, Black BLA, Blue BLU, Brown BRO, Gray GRY, Green GRN, Hazel HAZ, with Light shades printing as their base color. Heterochromia characters print both codes, left eye first (BLU/GRN). Random NPC cards stay vanilla for now. Might change once I give zombies eyes in a future update.
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com]Spongie's Character Customisation[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com]Tomb's Player Body[/url] - Their custom faces are drawn over the eye overlay, so the eyes and lashes are put back on top of them and show over custom faces too.
