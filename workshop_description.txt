[h1]Vanilla Eyes[/h1]

512x512 eye colors in 12 shades for the vanilla bodies, with selectable eye shape, eye height, and lash length. All art was handmade in Krita. Tried doing 256, but they all looked even more unnerving than these.

[b]Open Disclosure. I utilize several daemons through [i]opencode[/i] in my workflow for formatting, code analysis, debugging, and testing. [i]I'm not concerned about your opinion on that[/i], so keep it to yourself.[/b]

[h1]Contents[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Eye colors in 12 shades[/b] - Amber, Black, Blue, Brown, Gray, Green, Hazel, Light Amber, Light Blue, Light Brown, Light Green, Light Hazel - with separate male/female fits.
[*][b]Eye shapes[/b] - four shapes selected from the [b]Eye Shape[/b] dropdown.
[*][b]Eye heights[/b] - [b]Low[/b], [b]Normal[/b] (default), and [b]High[/b], so the eye sits where you want on the face.
[*][b]Lash lengths[/b] - [b]None[/b], [b]Short[/b], and [b]Long[/b]. Lashes are a separate layer drawn over the eye color.
[*][b]Layered rendering[/b] - face, then eyes, then lashes. Changing one layer never replaces another.
[*][b]Split left and right eyes[/b] - each color ships its own left-eye and right-eye art, worn on two separate slots.
[*][b]Heterochromia trait[/b] - a 1-point disadvantage that gives a character two different eye colors. Pick the trait and the single dropdown becomes [b]Left Eye Color[/b] and [b]Right Eye Color[/b]; the two can never match, not even from the randomize button.
[*][b]Creation dropdowns[/b] - pick your eyes right in the body section. Hover previews, the randomize button rolls eyes too, and outfit/gender/skintone changes keep your picks.
[/list]

[h1]Standalone[/h1]
Eyes and lashes are ordinary clothing worn at the mod's own hydeyes:lefteye and hydeyes:righteye slots, plus hydeyes:leftlash and hydeyes:rightlash for the lash layers - nothing else in the game uses them, so no glasses conflict, no masks, no makeup kits. They ride the creation desc into the game, then persist and sync like any other worn item. No requirements, no known incompatibilities, but may need small patches to show on other mod's 3d paperdoll models.

[h1]Functions[/h1]
All of this lives in [b]42/media/lua[/b]. Eye entries themselves are registered with [b]hydEyesData.AddEye[/b] (see the For Modders section).
[list]
[*][b]hydEyesData.AddEye[/b] - registers one eye color: male/female items, optional left/right split items, texture counts, and an optional short code label. The creation dropdown lists entries in registration order.
[*][b]hydEyesData.itemFor[/b] - picks the wearer's item for an entry: sex fit first, then the texture index clamped to what the item carries.
[*][b]hydEyesData.eyeTextureIndex[/b] / [b]lashTextureIndex[/b] - map the chosen shape, height, and lash length onto the item's base-texture list.
[*][b]hydEyesData.wearColors[/b] - wears the chosen color(s), shape, height, and lash length on any character: creation preview, customisation preview, or spawned player.
[*][b]Creation dropdowns[/b] - built under the body divider on first open. Hovering previews on the avatar, picking wears immediately, the dice rolls eyes too, and outfit/gender/skintone changes re-apply the picks.
[*][b]Play handoff[/b] - the preview copy is stripped before the world loads (so the engine never trips over it) and each spawned player is dressed in their recorded picks - per-player in splitscreen.
[*][b]hydEyesData.wearOn[/b] - the post-spawn dress call above; also reusable by other mods.
[/list]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]None[/b]. This mod is fully standalone. Should work on most retextures unless they put the eyes in a strange spot.
[*][b]Dedicated servers [i]must[/i] install this mod too.[/b] Clients need the textures.
[/list]

[h1]For Modders - Add your own eyes![/h1]
This pack is a framework: register entries with hydEyesData.AddEye and they appear in the creation dropdown automatically. Full guide with copy-paste snippets ships with the mod as [b]WIKI.md[/b]. Also available on the [url=https://github.com/hyd-n-plyn-syt/hydEyes]Github[/url].

[h1]Conditional compat patches[/h1]
No extra requirements, each activates only if its target mod is present.
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3815170396]GridCharacterHub[/url] - the health-view model keeps your eyes and lashes instead of dropping them, both the left and the right slot. Uses a [b]keepOnModel[/b] wrapper so the build keeps the eye and lash slots, plus a per-panel render hook ([b]reInjectGridEyes[/b]) that re-wears them onto the model's desc after every rebuild.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3796373365]ItIsOfInterestToMe[/url] - your ID card photo wears your eyes and lashes ([b]copyLiveEyes[/b] live plus a per-card snapshot so rebuilds survive reloads), and your own card prints the matching eye color in that mod's 3-letter codes ([b]wornEyeCodes[/b]): Amber AMB, Black BLA, Blue BLU, Brown BRO, Gray GRY, Green GRN, Hazel HAZ, with Light shades printing as their base color. Heterochromia characters print both codes, left eye first (BLU/GRN). Random NPC cards stay vanilla for now. Might change once I give zombies eyes in a future update.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3414634809]Spongie's Character Customisation[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3429790870]Tomb's Player Body[/url] - their custom faces are drawn over the eye overlay, so the eyes and lashes are put back on top of them and show over custom faces too.
[/list]

[h1]Other Compatibility[/h1]
[list]
[*]Multiplayer and splitscreen are [b]untested[/b] - single-player is verified. The code paths are set up to work (per-player picks keyed by player index, engine-synced worn clothing), but neither has been confirmed in a live session yet.
[*]Works with all vanilla skintones, and even some mods that change the body model itself, like Better Bodies.
[/list]
